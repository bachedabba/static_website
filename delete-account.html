Data Safety & Account Management

Request Account & Data Deletion

Bache Dabba is committed to transparent data handling and protecting your family's privacy in compliance with India's Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act 2023, Google Play Data Safety, and Apple App Store Guideline 5.1.1(v).

⚠️ Please Note: Deleting your account will immediately terminate all scheduled school meal deliveries, cancel active subscriptions, revoke secondary family access, and unlink assigned RFID/QR tags.

1 Delete Directly in the Mobile App (Instant)

If you have the Bache Dabba app installed on your device, you can delete your account and personal information directly without contacting support:

  1. Open the Bache Dabba app on your Android or iOS device and sign in.
  2. Tap the Profile tab on the bottom navigation bar (or your avatar icon on the top right).
  3. Scroll to the Account & Privacy section:
    • Select Delete Account to permanently close your account and cancel all meal deliveries.
  4. In the confirmation dialog, type DELETE in capital letters and confirm.
  5. Your active login session will be invalidated immediately, all subscriptions cancelled, and your account marked for deletion.

2 Request Deletion via Email (No App Required)

If you have uninstalled the app or no longer have access to your mobile device, submit the form below or email support@bachedabba.com with the subject "Account Deletion Request".

Data Handling, Deletion & Retention Policy

To ensure complete compliance with global privacy regulations and statutory tax laws, data associated with your account is processed as follows:

Data Category Action Upon Deletion Retention Period & Purpose
User Profile
(Name, Phone, Email, Avatar)		 Permanently Erased / Anonymized Immediately invalidated; personal identifiers scrubbed after 30-day cooling-off window.
Saved Addresses & Preferences
(Apartment, Society, City, Bag Type)		 Permanently Deleted Erased immediately upon account deletion or info deletion request.
Child & School Mapping
(School name, Class, Section, Bag Tag)		 Permanently Unlinked Tags returned to pool. Child identities in our system are already zero-PII pseudonymized (kid_1, kid_2).
Active Subscriptions & Tracking
(Daily lunch delivery runs)		 Terminated Daily delivery stops immediately. Real-time GPS location logs are purged.
Payment Invoices & Transaction IDs
(Razorpay payment IDs, GST invoice numbers)		 Retained (Anonymized) Mandatory retention for 7 to 8 years under Indian Companies Act & GST regulations for tax and financial auditing, decoupled from your name and phone.

Cooling-Off Period & Account Restoration

Account deletion requests include a 30-day grace period. During this window:

  • Your account remains disabled and all deliveries remain paused.
  • No one can access your profile or make unauthorized changes.
  • If you submitted the request in error or wish to reactivate your subscriptions, you can email support@bachedabba.com within 30 days to cancel the request.
  • After 30 days, all personal identifiers are permanently scrubbed and cannot be recovered.
