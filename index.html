Our Story

About Bache Dabba

We started Bache Dabba to solve a simple daily problem every working parent faces: how to get warm, home-cooked food to school without driving through morning traffic.

By collecting packed lunch boxes from apartment gate counters and delivering them directly to school lunch rooms, we save parents time and stress while keeping children healthy with home food.
