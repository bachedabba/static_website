Four steps, every school morning

Nothing changes in your kitchen. Only the school run does.

  1. Pack the dabba

    Cook and pack your child's lunch the way you always do.

  2. Leave it at the gate

    Drop it at the pickup counter by your apartment's security gate before 10:30 AM.

  3. We collect it

    A verified rider scans the QR tag on the box and takes it on the school route.

  4. School handover

    Your child gets it just before the lunch break.

Questions parents ask

Can't find your answer? Write to us at support@bachedabba.com

Which schools do you deliver to?

Currently, we started with schools in Miyapur and Bachupalli.

How does apartment pickup work?

Leave the QR-tagged lunch box at your community security gate before 10:30 AM. No QR tag, no pickup.

Can both parents track the lunch box?

Yes. Both parents can follow the status from pickup to school handover in the app.

Can I change my address or school?

Yes. Update it in the app and we'll confirm the new route with you.

How do you stop dabbas getting mixed up?

Every box carries a QR tag linked to your child's name and class. It is scanned at pickup and at school.

Why we started

This journey began from our own experiences as parents. We truly understand the daily challenges you face because we live them too.
